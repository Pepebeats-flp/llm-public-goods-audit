# Preregistration

**Study:** Incentives or wording? An audit of LLM decision rules in the linear public goods game
**Target venue:** Social Simulation Conference 2027
**Version:** 1.0, written 17 November 2026, before any data of the confirmatory run were collected
**Status:** DRAFT, not yet posted to a public registry

## 1. Disclosure: what was seen before writing this document

A pilot was run on 17 November 2026 (`pilot_20261117`: 6 models without reasoning, 6 values of the marginal per capita return, 7 prompt variants, 2 decisions per cell, 504 decisions and 72 comprehension probes). Its data were inspected. The pilot is excluded from all confirmatory analyses and is never pooled with the confirmatory data.

The pilot informed the following choices, which are therefore not independent of data:

- Hypothesis H6 (naming the game lowers contributions) was added after seeing the pilot.
- The variant with worked examples (P3) was reclassified from "paraphrase" to its own category after the pilot showed a large shift. A new pure paraphrase (P4) was written to replace it in the paraphrase set. P4 has not been run.
- The primary analysis treats contributions as nearly binary, because 86 to 100 percent of pilot decisions per model were 0 or 20.

Hypotheses H1 to H5 were written in the project guide before the pilot.

## 2. Research questions

- RQ1: Do contributions of LLM agents respond to the marginal per capita return (MPCR), and do the agents choose the dominant action where self-interest and collective welfare coincide?
- RQ2: How much of the variation in contributions is due to rewording the same game, compared with changing its payoffs and with reframing it?

Mixed populations, the repeated game and the strategy method (RQ3 and RQ4 of the guide) are outside this preregistration. If run, they will be registered separately or reported as exploratory.

## 3. Design

One-shot linear public goods game, N = 4, endowment E = 20, integer contribution c in 0..20, payoff u_i = E - c_i + (r/N) * sum(c). Each observation is one independent API call that returns one decision. There is no interaction between calls.

**Factors**

| Factor | Levels |
|---|---|
| MPCR m = r/N | 0.2 (control, r < 1), 0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.75 (dilemma), 1.25 (control, m > 1) |
| Prompt variant | P0 baseline; P1, P2, P4 paraphrases; P3 worked examples; F1 take frame; F2 "Teamwork" label; F3 named game |
| Model | gpt-5.6-luna, claude-haiku-5-5, qwen3.8-flash, glm-5.3-flash, deepseek-v4.1-flash, hy3 |
| Reasoning | off (`reasoning_effort: none`) for all six; on (`high`) for five models (all except gpt-5.6-luna) |

Fully crossed for reasoning off: 6 models × 6 MPCR × 8 variants × 50 decisions = 14,400 decisions. Reasoning on: 5 models × 6 MPCR × P0 only × 50 = 1,500 decisions. Comprehension probes (two payoff-calculation questions, separate calls): 20 per model and MPCR without reasoning, 10 with reasoning.

**Fixed materials.** Prompt texts are generated by `pgg/prompts.py`; configurations are the eleven files `configs/final_off_<model>.json` (reasoning off) and `configs/final_on_<model>.json` (reasoning on), one per model. Runs execute in parallel, one process per file; each decision is an independent call, so parallel execution does not change the design. An automated test checks that all variants imply identical payoffs. SHA-256 hashes of these files are in `preregistration_manifest.txt`.

**Models and access.** Models are accessed through the OpenCode Go inference API on the dates of the run. Canonical identifiers, open-weights status and prices are in `configs/opencode_go_models_snapshot.json` (retrieved 17 November 2026). Temperature is 1.0. The order of calls is shuffled with a fixed seed.

**Sample size rationale.** 50 decisions per cell gives a 95% interval half-width of at most 14 percentage points for a proportion in a single cell, and at most 5 points for a model-by-MPCR mean pooled over the four paraphrases (200 decisions). No formal power analysis was done. The number is fixed in advance; there is no optional stopping.

## 4. Outcomes

- **Primary:** contribution share s = c / E.
- **Secondary:** indicator of full contribution (c = 20); indicator of zero contribution (c = 0); correctness of comprehension answers; rate of invalid outputs.

For the take frame F1 the contribution is E minus the stated withdrawal.

## 5. Hypotheses and tests

All tests are run separately for each model. Unless stated, data are from the reasoning-off condition. Familywise error is controlled with the Holm procedure across the six models within each hypothesis, at alpha = 0.05. Confidence intervals are 95% percentile bootstrap intervals with 10,000 resamples of decisions within cells.

**H1a (incentive response).** Inside the dilemma range, contributions increase with MPCR.
Test: OLS of s on m (0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.75) with fixed effects for the four paraphrase variants (P0, P1, P2, P4); one-sided test of slope > 0 with heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors. Reported with the slope in percentage points per unit of m and Spearman's rho. Robustness: logistic regression of the full-contribution indicator on m.

**H1b (controls).** Agents choose the dominant action outside the dilemma.
Criterion, on the paraphrase set: mean s <= 0.10 at m = 0.2 and mean s >= 0.90 at m = 1.25. A model passes a control if the 95% interval lies entirely on the correct side of the threshold. The result is reported as pass, fail, or inconclusive for each control.

**H2 (paraphrase versus framing).** Variation across paraphrases is greater than zero and smaller than variation across framings.
Two balanced two-way layouts inside the dilemma range, per model:
- Paraphrase layout: 4 MPCR × {P0, P1, P2, P4}.
- Framing layout: 4 MPCR × {P0, F1, F2, F3}.

For each layout, eta-squared for MPCR, for wording, for their interaction and for the residual, from the sums of squares of the balanced design.
Tests: (i) main effect of wording in the paraphrase layout (F test); (ii) eta-squared of wording in the framing layout minus eta-squared of wording in the paraphrase layout greater than zero, by bootstrap interval.

**Headline descriptive measure:** signal-to-noise ratio = eta-squared of MPCR divided by eta-squared of wording in the paraphrase layout, with bootstrap interval. When the denominator is below 0.001 the ratio is reported as "> 100" rather than as a number.

**H5 (reasoning; exploratory).** With reasoning on, contributions at P0 are lower than with reasoning off at each MPCR inside the dilemma. Reported with differences and intervals; no confirmatory claim.

**H6 (named game; added after the pilot).** Relative to P0, naming the game (F3) lowers contributions inside the dilemma and at m = 1.25.
Test: difference in mean s between F3 and P0, two-sided, in each of the two regions. Because the hypothesis was formed after the pilot, it is labelled as such in the paper.

**Descriptive only:** P3 against P0; F1 and F2 against P0; distribution of decisions over 0, 10, 20 and other values; comprehension accuracy by item, model and MPCR; relation between comprehension accuracy and control performance.

## 6. Comparison with human evidence

Slopes are compared with published human results in direction and order of magnitude only: positive, and roughly 25 to 50 percentage points per unit of MPCR (Zelmer 2003; Isaac and Walker 1988; Gächter and Marino Fages 2026). No test against human data is performed, because designs differ. These reference values come from working-paper versions and will be checked against the published versions before submission.

## 7. Data exclusions and handling

- An output without a valid action is recorded as invalid and the cell is queried once more. If the second output is also invalid the cell is missing. Both rows are kept. Invalid-output rates are reported per model and variant.
- Transport failures (HTTP 429 or 5xx, timeouts) are retried up to three times and are not model outputs.
- No decision is removed as an outlier. No output is repaired.
- If a model becomes unavailable or the provider interrupts access before its cells are complete, the model is reported with the data collected, flagged as incomplete, and excluded from tests that need the balanced layout.
- If a model rejects the temperature parameter, the provider default is used and reported.

## 8. Known limitations fixed in advance

- Access is through an intermediary. The served model is recorded as reported by the provider and cannot be verified independently.
- The game is well known; training-data recall is a rival explanation for behaviour. The neutral baseline, the two controls and variant F3 are the design's response to it.
- Results concern six models on the dates of the run, one group size and one endowment.
- No counterfactual or policy conclusion will be drawn from the behaviour of LLM agents.

## 9. Deviations

Any deviation from this plan will be listed in the paper with its reason.
